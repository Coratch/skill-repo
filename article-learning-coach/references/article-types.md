# Article Types

Adjust the workflow by source type.

## Research Paper

Prioritize:

- Research question
- Prior work gap
- Method
- Dataset or sample
- Experiment/design
- Results
- Threats to validity
- Reproducibility details
- Contribution vs evidence

Use this table:

| Section | Learning focus |
|---|---|
| Abstract | Claimed contribution |
| Introduction | Problem and gap |
| Related Work | Positioning |
| Method | Mechanism and assumptions |
| Experiments | Evidence quality |
| Discussion | Scope and limitations |
| Conclusion | Claimed takeaway |

Be strict about causal claims, benchmarks, sample size, and missing baselines.

## Technical Blog

Prioritize:

- Problem context
- Architecture or implementation pattern
- Tradeoffs
- Failure modes
- Operational lessons
- What is reusable vs context-specific

Extract "how to apply this" and "when not to apply this".

## Business or Strategy Essay

Prioritize:

- Market or organizational context
- Strategic claim
- Incentives and constraints
- Evidence quality
- Hidden assumptions
- Decision implications

Watch for overgeneralization from anecdotes.

## Product Article

Prioritize:

- User problem
- Product bet
- Workflow impact
- Metrics or adoption evidence
- UX or GTM implication
- Competitive positioning

Translate insights into product decisions, risks, and experiments.

## Report or Whitepaper

Prioritize:

- Sponsor and incentive
- Methodology
- Data source
- Definitions
- Quantitative claims
- Uncertainty
- Policy or business recommendations

Separate measured data from interpretation.
